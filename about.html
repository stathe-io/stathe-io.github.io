Jarno Kristelijn

Platform & DevSecOps engineer · Netherlands

I build production environments for business-critical systems, where availability and controlled change are not negotiable. Mostly that means turning fragmented delivery into pipelines you can trace: continuous delivery, infrastructure as code, GitOps. Some of it is architecture — designing secure delivery platforms for high-security environments, covering pipeline design, artifact signing and supply chain integrity.

I spent over a decade in QA before moving into platform work, and it shows in how I build. Ten years of asking how and why systems fail turns out to be the best possible preparation for making sure they don't. I still work the same way: test-driven, in small increments, and sceptical of anything I have not watched fail for the right reason.
